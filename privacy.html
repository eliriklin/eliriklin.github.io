Privacy Policy - The Grain Desk

Last updated: November 3, 2026

What this application is

The Grain Desk publishing application is used only by the owner of The Grain Desk YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos, thumbnails, captions and comments, and to read view statistics for those videos.

Data we access

  • Through the YouTube Data API, with the channel owner's authorization: permission to upload videos and manage the channel's own videos, captions and comments.
  • Public view, like and comment counts of the channel's own videos.

Data we do not collect

The application does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of YouTube viewers or any other users. It does not use Google user data for advertising.

Storage and security

The authorization token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and is used solely to perform the actions above. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This application uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through its YouTube "About" page.
