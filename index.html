THE GRAIN DESK

Ag markets, explained with data.

The Grain Desk is a YouTube channel that explains U.S. agricultural commodity markets - corn, soybeans, wheat, cattle and hogs - using official public data from the CFTC Commitments of Traders report, USDA's WASDE and the U.S. Drought Monitor.

This site belongs to the channel's publishing application, which uploads the channel's own videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. It does not access any other user's data.

Content is for information only and is not financial advice.
